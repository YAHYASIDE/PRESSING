# Washly — User Guide

A practical guide for the people who use Washly day to day: cashiers, workers and
managers. Washly is mobile-first — everything below works on a phone.

## 1. First launch & sign in
1. On first launch the **Setup Wizard** opens (4 quick steps): choose your
   business activities, enter the **manager** name/phone/password (this creates
   your account and logs you in), enter the business name/logo, then Finish.
   You get a **free 3-day trial** with all premium features.
2. After that, every launch shows the **lock screen**: enter your name, pick your
   role (Manager / Cashier / Worker), and enter the access code.

## 2. The bottom navigation
Tabs appear based on your **role** and the **features/business types** enabled:
Home (Operations Center), POS, Operations (cars), Customers, Carpets, Expenses,
Reports, Accounting, Inventory. Workers see only Operations.

## 3. Operations Center (Home)
The dashboard opens on a live **Operations Center**: today's revenue / profit /
expenses / orders, the car / carpet / oil queues, ready-for-pickup, cash & bank
balances, low-stock count, and a **reminders** panel (oil due, low stock, debts,
trial). Below it are the period charts and reports.

## 4. Point of Sale (POS)
1. **البيع (Sell):** tap products (from inventory) or services to add them to the
   cart. Use the search or category chips to find items fast.
2. Tap the floating **cart bar** to open the cart: adjust quantities, add a
   **discount** (amount or %), apply a **coupon**, set the customer name/plate,
   and add a note. Tax is applied automatically if enabled.
3. **Payment:** tap a method to pay the remaining balance; add several methods to
   **split** the payment. Any unpaid remainder becomes **store credit (آجل)**.
4. **إتمام البيع** posts the sale (accounting + inventory update automatically),
   shows the **receipt** (with QR) to print, and clears the cart.
5. **تعليق (Hold)** parks the cart; resume it later from **الفواتير (Invoices)**.
6. From **Invoices** you can reprint 🧾, duplicate ⧉ or **refund** ↩ (full or
   partial — refunds restock products and reverse the accounting).

## 5. Car operations
- Tap **＋** to receive a car (vehicle, plate, phone, service, price, photos).
- Tap an operation to open its **Details** screen: one large guided button moves
  it through the workflow (Start Washing → Finish → Ready → Collect & Deliver).
- Delivery asks for the payment method and closes the operation.

## 6. Customers (CRM) & vehicles
- **Customers** lists everyone; tap a customer for their profile: total spending,
  visits, average ticket, outstanding balance, a unified **timeline** (invoices,
  oil changes, car & carpet orders, refunds), their **vehicles**, and contact
  info. Edit ✏️ any field.
- Add vehicles (plate, brand, model, mileage, oil type…). Each vehicle keeps a
  **service history**.

## 7. Oil change
On a vehicle (oil-change businesses), tap **🛢️ تغيير زيت**: pick the oil from
inventory + quantity, add a filter, enter labor price and the odometer reading,
and the next-change interval. Confirming: consumes the oil/filters, posts the
invoice, updates the vehicle mileage & next-oil target, and creates a reminder.

## 8. Inventory
- **Products** tab: add products (name, category, cost, price, quantity, reorder
  point, barcode, expiry). Cards show stock status and expiry.
- **＋ استلام** receives stock, **جرد** adjusts a physical count, **تعديل** edits.
- **تنبيهات** shows low stock + expiring items; **الموردون** manages suppliers;
  **الحركات** is the movement history.

## 9. Reports & export
The **Reports** tab has Financial, Sales, Customers, Inventory and Analytics
sub-tabs over the selected date range. Each report has a **⬇️ تصدير CSV** button
(opens in Excel) and the header 🖨️ prints.

## 10. Global search
Tap 🔍 in the header to search everything at once — customers, vehicles,
invoices, operations, products, suppliers, journal entries — and jump straight
to any result.

## 11. Tips
- Amounts show your configured **currency**. Change it (and services, payment
  methods, tax, working hours) in **Settings ▸ إعدادات النشاط**.
- The app works **offline**; data is saved on the device.
- Use **Logout ⎋** to hand the device to another user/role.
